About the Department

01Engineering the Future of Semiconductor Technology

The Department of Electronics Engineering – VLSI Design and Technology (EE–VDT) at Sri Shakthi Institute of Engineering and Technology, Coimbatore, is dedicated to developing the next generation of semiconductor engineers, chip designers and technology innovators.

The department focuses on building strong foundations in digital electronics, semiconductor devices, VLSI architecture, RTL design, verification, FPGA development and physical design. Through structured academic learning, practical laboratory sessions, technical workshops and project-based education, students gain exposure to the complete journey of integrated circuit development — from design concepts to silicon implementation.

With an emphasis on emerging semiconductor technologies, the department encourages students to explore innovative solutions in low-power VLSI, ASIC design, embedded systems, IoT, signal processing and intelligent hardware architectures.

Our learning environment promotes technical curiosity, collaborative research, hands-on experimentation and continuous skill development. Through VLSI-focused training programmes, research activities, hackathons and industry-oriented technical initiatives, we strive to bridge the gap between academic knowledge and real-world semiconductor engineering.

Core Purpose

02Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To develop competent semiconductor professionals and innovative engineers capable of contributing to the advancement of VLSI design, integrated circuits and next-generation electronic technologies.

Excellence in Silicon Engineering

Our Mission

  • 1 Establish strong fundamentals in semiconductor technology, digital electronics and VLSI design.
  • 2 Provide practical exposure to industry-standard EDA tools, FPGA platforms and chip design methodologies.
  • 3 Encourage research, innovation and real-time hardware development.
  • 4 Promote technical collaboration through workshops, hackathons, projects and professional learning communities.
  • 5 Prepare students for emerging opportunities in the global semiconductor and electronics industries.
