About the Department

Where Curiosity Meets
Circuit Design

Established in 2024, the Department of VLSI Design and Technology at SIET is committed to building strong fundamentals in VLSI, Embedded Systems, and semiconductor design - preparing students for the evolving chip industry through hands-on labs and industry-aligned curriculum.

  • Active research in IoT, low-power VLSI, and antenna systems
  • Industry MoUs for internships, live projects, and placement drives
  • Student chapters: IEEE, IETE, and active departmental clubs

Our Direction

Vision & Mission

Vision

To be a globally recognised centre of excellence in VLSI Design and Technology - producing technically competent, ethically grounded, and socially responsible engineers who contribute to society through innovation.

2030

Global Benchmark Goal

Top 50

National VLSI Ranking Target

Mission

  • 1 Deliver rigorous, application-focused curricula aligned with industry and research needs.
  • 2 Foster research, innovation, and entrepreneurship through state-of-the-art labs and industry collaboration.
  • 3 Develop graduates with strong professional ethics, communication skills, and a habit of lifelong learning.
  • 4 Build an inclusive environment where every student realises their full technical and personal potential.
