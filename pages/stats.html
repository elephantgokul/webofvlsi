Data Source & Methodology

All metrics are computed in real-time from the department's live student and faculty database via the Google Apps Script API.

  • Student counts: Direct enumeration from API response
  • Achievement parsing: Keyword frequency analysis on achievement text (same algorithm as Leaderboard)
  • Technology mentions: Case-insensitive pattern matching for 50+ VLSI tools, languages, and methodologies
  • Year distribution: Normalized from student year tokens (I/II/III/IV)
  • Profile completeness: Percentage of students with LinkedIn/GitHub URLs in their records
